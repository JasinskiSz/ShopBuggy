👋 Our Story

Built by shoppers,
for shoppers.

ShopBuggy started in a garage in 2021. Today we serve over 850,000 happy customers across 38 countries.

Our Mission

We believe great products shouldn't come with a premium price tag. Our mission is to connect consumers with quality goods from trusted sellers worldwide — quickly, safely, and affordably.

From day one we've been obsessed with the customer experience: fast shipping, straightforward returns, and a team that actually picks up the phone. We're proud to say 99.2% of our customers rate their experience 4 stars or higher.
